# Puddlewick

**[Play in your browser](https://ivan2000w.github.io/puddlewick/)**

Enter the game passcode supplied by your host. For two players, select **Multiplayer → Host room** and send the invite to a friend. Your friend unlocks the game and selects **Join room**. Keep the host’s tab open while you play.

Meet up on Wrong Turn Terrace, drive around Cornflake Crescent, or run a delivery from Lunchbox School to the club workshop together.

Walk into **Corner Basket** for the club shopping list, Rocket Fizz and Moon Pops. Visit **Pepper Petrol** for Comet fuel, **Second Wind Gym** for cardio and weights, or **Stretch Club** for a reformer class. All four are on the in-game map.
